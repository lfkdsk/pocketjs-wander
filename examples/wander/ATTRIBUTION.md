# Asset attribution — endless wander

The full, current asset attribution for this repository (Ninja Adventure
CC0, Tiny 16 CC-BY, generated composites CC0) lives at the repository
root: [ATTRIBUTION.md](../../ATTRIBUTION.md).

The wander world draws the rule-grown settlement's committed PNGs in
`vendor/pocket-rpgkit/examples/grow/assets/`, referenced in place (the build
resolves `../../vendor/pocket-rpgkit/examples/grow/assets/<file>.png`
against this directory), plus composites of those same PNGs and its own
character look pool. The full provenance of the grow art is in
`vendor/pocket-rpgkit/examples/grow/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
